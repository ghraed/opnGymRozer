# Drop sets

1. Open **Plan**, then your routine.
2. Tap the **down-arrow icon** next to the superset link to mark an exercise as a drop set.
3. Tap the exercise and configure its drop rows and individual weights, then **Save**.
4. Start a new workout. Only the configured drop rows appear: for example, two drop rows replace an exercise's three regular sets. Enter actual reps and complete the rows without resting between them.
5. Turn off the down-arrow flag to use the original regular sets and weights again.

Regular sets and their settings are retained separately and hidden while the drop-set flag is on. Drop weights do not inherit the regular exercise's weight. New rows start at zero or suggest a reduction from the preceding drop weight; enter the weights you intend to use.

Workout weight edits save immediately to the corresponding drop row in the routine, including lower weights and zero. They survive reloads and do not overwrite the regular-set weights. Freestyle edits remain in the active session only. Completed workout history is unchanged.

Drop-only sessions do not drive regular-set progression or replace the regular working-weight default. They still count toward workout volume and appear in history. Plan sharing preserves both configurations, and the plan summary displays only the active configuration.

Routine changes apply to new workouts; an already-started workout keeps its session configuration. During a marked workout, **Add drop** adds a session-only drop, and **Remove set** removes its last row. Regular **Add set** is hidden while marked.
